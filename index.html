№ 02 · Their Story

A tale written in the stars

Every great love story deserves a grand beginning, and ours began at a friend's sangeet — a shared plate of jalebis, one borrowed shawl, and a conversation that outlasted the whole playlist.

Six years, two cities and a thousand cups of chai later, we are writing our next chapter in the oldest language we know — love. Nothing would mean more than to have you beside us when we do.
